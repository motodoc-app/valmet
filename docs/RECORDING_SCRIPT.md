# Recording script — 2-minute submission video (Industrial X-Ray)

Organisers' format: **Problem 0:00–0:15 · Solution 0:15–0:35 · Live demo 0:35–1:35 · Business impact 1:35–2:00**,
voice narration + English subtitles. Subtitles for exactly these lines: [`video/subtitles-v2.srt`](video/subtitles-v2.srt).

## Before you press record (5 minutes)
1. Laptop plugged in, Chrome, screen 1920×1080 (or your laptop's native size), browser zoom 100 %, notifications off.
2. Open the live demo: **https://claude.ai/artifact/FbUbNXsimhQWK9Zw9Vpebs** and make the browser full screen (F11 /
   Ctrl+Cmd+F). Wait until the room is sharp (all 18 scan parts loaded, about 10–30 s).
3. Close the "How to use" card and the "Take the 1-minute tour" invite if they show.
4. Do one dry run of the clicks below, then reload the page (or rail → **Reset the whole demo**) so the robot is back
   at its dock.
5. Start your recorder (OBS, or Cmd+Shift+5 on Mac / Win+Alt+R on Windows). Speak calmly: the lines below take
   about 1:50 at a normal pace.

## Shot list — what to click and what to say

| 0:47–1:02 | | Ask *Send the robot to P-005*. Bottom bar: **Speed** to 4×, then **Robot POV**. (Optional: **Block aisle** — it re-routes.) | "The robot plans a collision-free way in. This is its camera — simulated from the scan, not a live feed. Block the aisle and it finds a detour." |
| 1:02–1:17 | | On arrival: *You've arrived at P-005* → **Inspect equipment**. Drag the **Explode** slider; suspect parts are red. Press **×**. | "It stops in front of the pump and turns to face it. | 1:17–1:35 | | Top tab **Plan** → click the **pallet truck** → drag it clear (or **Suggest a good spot for me**). ## Don't say
"AI recognised 676 machines" · "live robot camera" · "real plant data" · "certified clearance" · "proven savings" ·
"faster/cheaper than Unity" · any connection to DNAe or plant controls (there is none).
