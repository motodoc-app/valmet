# Recording script — 2-minute submission video (Industrial X-Ray)

Organisers' format: **Problem 0:00–0:15 · Solution 0:15–0:35 · Live demo 0:35–1:35 · Business impact 1:35–2:00**,
voice narration + English subtitles. Subtitles for exactly these lines: [`video/subtitles-v2.srt`](video/subtitles-v2.srt).

## Before you press record (5 minutes)
1. Laptop plugged in, Chrome, screen 1920×1080 (or your laptop's native size), browser zoom 100 %, notifications off.
2. Open the live demo: **https://claude.ai/artifact/FbUbNXsimhQWK9Zw9Vpebs** and make the browser full screen (F11 /
   Ctrl+Cmd+F). Wait until the room is sharp (all 18 scan parts loaded, about 10–30 s).
3. Close the "How to use" card and the "Take the 1-minute tour" invite if they show.
4. Do one dry run of the clicks below, then reload the page (or rail → **Reset the whole demo**) so the robot is back
   at its dock.
5. Easiest: record the screen first without talking (OBS, or Cmd+Shift+5 on Mac / Win+Alt+R on Windows), following the
   Click column; then record your voice over it in CapCut (or similar) reading the Say column. About 2:00 at a calm pace.

## Shot list — what to click and what to say

| Time | Part | Click / show | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | **Problem** | The room at eye level. Slowly drag to look around. | "Technicians often arrive at an unfamiliar plant without knowing where the equipment is, what's wrong with it, or how to reach it. That uncertainty costs time before work even begins." |
| 0:15–0:35 | **Solution** | Right rail → **Scene understanding** (check-mark icon): the tiers light up in 3D. Close it. | "Meet Industrial X-Ray. Using Valmet's 3D scan and equipment records, we build an interactive copy of the facility, so technicians can explore, locate assets and prepare before they travel. From the scan we segment 676 object candidates — and show honestly how many are confidently identified." |
| 0:35–0:50 | **Live demo** | Click **Ask about the plant**, type *What's wrong with P-005?*, Enter. The diagnosis card opens. | "Here we ask about pump P-005. The copilot gathers telemetry, alarms and work orders from the synthetic workbook: likely fouling, with every source cited." |
| 0:50–1:05 |  | Ask *Send the robot to P-005*. Bottom bar: **Speed** to 4×, **Robot POV**, then **Block aisle** — it re-routes. | "We dispatch our simulated inspection robot and switch to its camera — rendered from the scan, not a live feed. When we block the aisle, it calculates a new route." |
| 1:05–1:20 |  | On arrival: *You've arrived at P-005* → **Inspect equipment**. Drag the **Explode** slider; suspect parts are red. Press **×**. | "It stops in front of the pump and faces it. We open an exploded teaching model right inside the scene to understand the components. Close it, and we're exactly where we were." |
| 1:20–1:35 |  | Top tab **Plan** → **Suggest a good spot for me** (or click the pallet truck and drag it clear). The card says **It fits**. | "In Plan mode we move the pallet truck out of the way. Collisions, clearance and the robot's route update instantly." |
| 1:35–2:00 | **Business impact** | Top tab **Value**: move one slider. End on the room. | "Industrial X-Ray brings spatial understanding, navigation, diagnostic evidence and training into one workflow, so technicians arrive better prepared. Today's data is synthetic. A pilot would measure the time saved, and could later connect — read-only — to systems such as Valmet DNAe." |
