# Scene understanding: what was actually recognised

_Generated by `python scripts/scene_quality.py` from `web/public/static/scene.json`. Numbers here are exact counts; no accuracy figure in this document is human-verified unless it says so._

## Plain-English summary

> 676 is the number of segmented 3D candidates, not the number of correctly recognised physical objects. Candidates include fragments of one object (pipe runs split into pieces), small tags and clutter. Confidence is the vision model's own estimate and is not calibrated; only a few objects have been checked by a person. Workbook links are procedural to synthetic records.

We should say: *"The pipeline segments the scan into 676 object candidates with oriented 3D frames; 418 get a specific equipment type, 197 of those with confidence >= 0.70; 30 are linked to the (synthetic) workbook assets."* We should not compare 676 with another team's object count: the counting units differ (fragments, tags and clutter are candidates here), and no one has measured precision against ground truth yet.

## Structure surfaces (reported separately)

## Workbook linking

30 candidates are linked to 30 workbook assets (one-to-one). Methods: class-aware × 29, class-aware (target, reachable) × 1. Mean mapping confidence 0.64; 14 of 30 have mapping confidence ≥ 0.70. The workbook is synthetic (challenge builder pack); bindings are procedural and class-aware (backend/xray/mapping.py), not field-verified. Mapping confidence = visual confidence x tier weight.

## Data-quality audit of candidates

385 of 676 candidates trigger at least one rule below; 291 trigger none. Rules overlap (one object can be tiny, flat and low-support at once). Flags are review hints, not verdicts.

| Flag | Count | Rule |
|---|---:|---|
| fragment | 58 | same specific label and oriented frames overlapping or within 0.03 m (union-find groups) |
| possible_overmerge | 11 | volume >= 0.5 m^3, or largest extent >= 1.2 m with elongation < 4.0 |
| tiny | 115 | largest extent < 0.15 m |
| flat | 112 | smallest extent at the 0.04 m frame clamp (thinner than ~4 cm) |
| low_points | 178 | fewer than 50 raw splats in the cluster |
| label_size_mismatch | 30 | small-device label (control valve, flow meter, junction box, manual valve, pipe label / tag, pressure gauge, pressure transmitter, temperature sensor, valve actuator) on a frame >= 0.6 m long |
| nested | 55 | centre lies inside a larger candidate's frame (likely a part of it) |

**Fragments / duplicates.** 25 groups of same-label touching frames contain 58 candidates; merging each group would remove 33 candidates. Groups by label: pipe label / tag 8, insulated pipe (horizontal) 6, pipe elbow / fitting 2, insulated pipe (vertical) 2, electric motor 2, pump-motor unit 1, control panel 1, valve actuator 1, temperature sensor 1, control valve 1. Largest groups: OBJ-005, OBJ-009, OBJ-062, OBJ-143, OBJ-235; OBJ-100, OBJ-148, OBJ-175, OBJ-612; OBJ-021, OBJ-469, OBJ-501; OBJ-041, OBJ-528, OBJ-621; OBJ-239, OBJ-462, OBJ-634.

## Label audit (AI second opinion — NOT human ground truth)

Design: stratified by label, proportional allocation, >= 1 per label, seed 20261107; population: 416 objects with a specific VLM label (label_source 'vlm+geometry'). A second model (Claude) looked at the same crop renders the labeller saw (context + close-up with the magenta 3D frame) and recorded agree / disagree / can't tell with a one-line reason (`docs/label_audit_ai.csv`). It shares the labeller's handicap (sparse splat renders), so this measures *plausibility to a second model*, not correctness.

_Audit incomplete: 60 of 60 sampled objects have no AI verdict yet._

### Real precision check at the event (human)

`docs/label_review_sample.csv` lists the same sample (id, label, confidence, stratum, crop path) with an empty `human_verdict` column. Open each crop (`data/processed/crops/<id>.jpg`, or select the id in the app), write agree / disagree / cant_tell, and compute agree/n with a Wilson interval. Only that number may be presented as precision.

## How to talk about it

- Lead with the method (unsupervised 3D segmentation + VLM labels with provenance + human review layer), then show the funnel, not the 676.
- Point at the confidence filter and review layer as the honest part: every label carries its source and confidence, and corrections are logged.
- Do not quote a percentage accuracy; quote the AI-audit interval only as an AI cross-check, and offer the CSV for a live human check.
